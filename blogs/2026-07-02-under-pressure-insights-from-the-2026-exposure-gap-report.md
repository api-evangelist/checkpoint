Check Point's 2026 Exposure Gap Report finds vulnerabilities now make up 42.6% of critical exposure, up sharply from 18.7% in 2025. The report stresses that only a small percentage of vulnerability alerts are validated as exploitable, underscoring the need for context-driven prioritization in security response.
